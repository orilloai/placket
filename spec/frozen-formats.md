# The register of frozen formats

One row per format of protocol version 0.1. A format is frozen once it is released: from then on a change to it is a new version of it, with a note in its row and in `vectors/README.md`, in the same change. Before release, a format may still change within 0.1; its row says how it stands, and a change carries a note in the row. A new type URI, reason or code, and a member written that was absent, are each a format change.

Every format here is at version `0.1`, and every one is unreleased. Every placket URI carries that one protocol number, the card's `spec` (setup-contract-4): each type URI ends in it, and each schema's `$id` holds it after `schema/`. No schema is fetched: each `$id` is under `https://placket.tech/schema/0.1/`, and `make ci` loads them from this directory.

## Notes

- 0.1, before release: the answer call joins the register. It is the door's signed call that accepts or changes an answer, which travels beside each delivery whose body is that answer and which a party keeps as evidence of who answered; so its signed members are fixed from this note on, as its row lists them. No other format changes, and no receipt, hash or vector changes with it. The door's other formats, the call of every other op, the reply, the journal and the state directory, are not frozen.
- 0.1, before release: a principal per party. In the founding set, the policy's `principals` is a list of entries, each a `principal` and the `roles` it may grant, in place of a list of actor references; a binding gains `policy`, the typed hash of the set's policy it was signed for, under the same type URI. Founding gains two codes, `binding-role` and `binding-policy`, in two rows after the binding's signature. A typed hash now also names a binding's policy. So every founding set, every binding and every Accord id changes, and with them every scenario vector's receipts and ids; no receipt, envelope, delivery or other format changes, and no other code, verdict or reason. The vector format's list of founding codes gains the two codes with them. Rows: Binding, Founding set, Typed hash, Reasons and codes.
- 0.1, before release: one host, one protocol number, 32-byte salts and A2A's meaning of role (decision record 0020). Every schema's `$id`, the three type URIs of signing-4, the receipt's predicate type and the crossing extension's URI move to `https://placket.tech/`, with the path and the number `0.1` as they were; every one carries the card's `spec`. Signing-4 names three type URIs: the reserved registration type is dropped. A salt, in a part, a delivery and a vector's `resolve` step, is exactly 32 bytes, 64 lowercase hex digits, where it was at least 16. A member's give has `role` `ROLE_USER`, and a part the mediator forms `ROLE_AGENT`; the door refuses a member's give with `ROLE_AGENT` with `extension-missing`. No code, verdict or reason is added. Every signature by an actor, every receipt, every Accord id and every vector changes. Rows: Signing construction and type URIs, Card, Crossing message and part, Delivery, Receipt, Conformance vector.
- 0.1, before release: who decided, the answer call, judges and conformance (decision record 0021). Every predicate gains `by`, required (receipt-11): the terms, a judge by its card name, an answer by its call's typed hash, or the mediator with `deadline` or `undeliverable`; the verifier's statement row checks that it fits the receipt, and its release row that a release repeats its closing's. The reason `judge:<name>`, on a crossing block only, replaces the reserved `check:<name>`, which no receipt may carry. The answer call is a frozen format of its own (`spec/schema/answer.json`, maybe-11), signed under a fourth type URI, `https://placket.tech/answer/0.1` (signing-4); it replaces the door's call that this register held before, which is no format of the protocol now. A delivery gains two optional members, `answer`, the call, and `mediaType`; a part gains an optional `mediaType`. A term's `resolve` gains an optional `judges`; the card's `judges` is the ordered list of the judges a term may grant, with unique names, and the card and the term lose `checks`. A policy gains an optional `supersedes`. Founding's row 8 is `judge-unknown`, and `check-unknown` is retired; section 6's table gains `answer-call` and `answer-body`, and the answer's misuse codes `action`, `salt` and `answer` are retired; verify-1 adds the conformance codes `founding`, `accord`, `party`, `decision`, `witness` and `body`. The vector format gains the operation and group `conform`, a give step's `judge`, and a resolve step of two shapes, an answer step holding `callHex` with `answer` and `salt`, or the mediator's finding holding `question` and `undeliverable`; a step holds no `action`, `content` or `release`, and the pairing of an answer step's action, outcome and release, which the schema can no longer read inside `callHex`, is the runner's. composition-4 is a reserved id. Every receipt, Accord id and scenario vector changes. Retired or renamed schema examples: `card/refused/check-loaded.json`, renamed `checks-member.json`; `term/accepted/with-a-check.json`, replaced by `maybe-with-judges.json`; `term/refused/checks-empty.json` and `check-without-corpus.json`, replaced by `checks-member.json`; `predicate/accepted/crossing-block-check.json`, renamed `crossing-block-judge.json`; and of the vector format, `resolve-accepted-without-salt.json`, `resolve-changed-empty-answer.json`, `resolve-release-on-declined.json`, `resolve-release-false.json`, `resolve-expect-release-without-release.json`, `resolve-accepted-outcome-declined.json` and `resolve-declined-outcome-cancelled.json`, retired since each existed for a conditional on `action`, `content` or `release` that is gone; `resolve-action-unknown.json`, renamed `resolve-with-action.json`, refused for holding `action` at all; and a new `resolve-callhex-and-undeliverable.json`. `resolve-salt-31-bytes.json` keeps its name. Retired or renamed vectors, which `vectors/README.md` notes too: `founding/a-check-and-maybe-without-resolve.json`, renamed `a-judge-and-maybe-without-resolve.json`; `founding/a-check-and-no-binding.json`, renamed `a-judge-and-no-binding.json`; `receipts/a-reserved-check-reason.json`, renamed `a-retired-check-reason.json`; and `questions/a-cancel.json`, a cancel by the first answerer that is now `not-an-answerer`, merged into `questions/cancel-by-asker.json` and retired. Rows: Signing construction and type URIs, Declaration and term, Card, Founding set, Crossing message and part, Delivery, Receipt, Reasons and codes, Conformance vector, Answer call.
- 0.1, before release: the close (decision record 0022). The register gains the close receipt, a format of its own (`spec/schema/close.json`, close-2): a DSSE envelope as a receipt's, with one signature by the mediator, whose statement's predicate type is `https://placket.tech/close/0.1` and whose one subject is the Accord, `{"name":"accord","digest":{"sha256":<the Accord id's hex>}}`; its predicate is exactly `accord`, `card`, `mediator`, `at`, `chains` and `unfetched`. A receipt predicate gains the reason `closed`, on a resolution `block` only, and the decider `{"mediator":"close"}`, each allowed only with the other (receipt-4, receipt-11); a closing with reason `closed` names the question's subject (receipt-6). The verifier gains two rows, `close` and `after-close`; the door's give and answer tables gain the rejection `closed`, right after membership. The vector format gains a scenario step `close`, a `get` step's expected `close`, the rejection `closed`, the codes `close` and `after-close` for `verify`, `reconcile` and `conform` vectors, and the group `close`. A door's reply to a fetch gains a member `close`, which is a door format and not frozen. No existing receipt, id or vector changes, and no format of the founding set: `supersedes` is read as setup-contract-11 already gives it. New schema examples: `close/accepted/chains-and-unfetched.json` and `an-accord-with-no-receipts.json`, ten refused close examples, `predicate/accepted/resolution-closed.json`, `predicate/refused/crossing-closed.json`, `closed-by-deadline.json` and `declined-by-close.json`, and `statement/refused/a-close-statement.json`. Rows: Receipt, Reasons and codes, Conformance vector, Close receipt.
